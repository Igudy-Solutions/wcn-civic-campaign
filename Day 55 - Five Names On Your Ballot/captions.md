## Instagram caption

Quick test: can you name the five people you'll vote for?

On Saturday 16 January 2027:

On Saturday 6 February 2027:

Five different jobs. The President runs the federal government. Senators and Reps make federal laws and pass the budget. Your Governor runs your state's schools, hospitals and roads. Your State Assembly member makes your state's laws and checks the governor.

Most people only think about one of the five. Know all five before you go.
