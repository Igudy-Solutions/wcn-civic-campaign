# Special, Parties On The Ballot

**Format** Single image
**Status** built, for team review
**Built by** `tools/parties_single.py`

Every party on the 2027 presidential ballot, with its candidate and running
mate, in INEC's ballot order. Every party gets the same tile; only the colour
chip changes. Ticket A has no candidate on INEC's final list (remark: court
order).

## Check before posting

- Party chip colours come from `tools/parties.py` and are not confirmed yet.
- Names are INEC's, printed surname first on the list; the order shown here
  (first name first) is our reading. Check spellings and order, especially
  for the less known candidates.
- If the court order on ticket A is resolved, update `data/presidential_candidates.json`.

## Sources

- INEC, Final List of Candidates, Presidential (PDF in `data/`, 30 Oct 2026)
