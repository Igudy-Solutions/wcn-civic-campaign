The WCNCountdown files are display adaptations of the bundled Sora variable
font. Source authors and SIL Open Font License remain recorded in the fonts
and in ../Sora-OFL.txt. The original ../Sora.ttf is unchanged.

Fixed weights: 500, 600, 800. Overlapping contours are merged. At weight 800,
the Y shoulder steps and G crossbar overhang are smoothed to avoid the
appearance of cracks in the large countdown label. Glyph advances are
preserved. Adapted fonts are named WCN Countdown rather than Sora.

Rebuild with ../../_tools/prepare_fonts.py. The native font naming records,
including copyright and license records, are preserved except for the
adapted family/style names.
