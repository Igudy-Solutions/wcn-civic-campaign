## Instagram caption

A slogan is not a plan.

Slogans are built to be catchy, loud and everywhere. Before you repeat one, ask three questions:

How will it be done?
With what money?
By when?

If there's no answer, there's no plan. Ask every candidate. Ask every party. Then choose for yourself.
