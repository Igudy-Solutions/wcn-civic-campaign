## Instagram caption

Your PVC is ready. Is it still sitting at INEC? 🪪

Registering was step one. Without the card in your hand, you can't vote on 16 January or 6 February. No PVC, no vote.

How to collect it:
1️⃣ Go to the INEC office in your local government area, or the collection point INEC announces for your ward
2️⃣ Take your registration slip, or your name, date of birth and phone number
3️⃣ Go in person. Nobody can collect it for you
4️⃣ It's free. No one should charge you for your own card

Before you leave, check your name and photo. Your polling unit is on the card too.

Collect it this week, and bring a friend. Tag the person whose PVC is still at INEC. 👇
