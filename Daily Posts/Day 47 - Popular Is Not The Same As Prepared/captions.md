## Instagram caption

Popular is not the same as prepared.

Crowds, jingles and trending hashtags tell you who is loud, not who is ready. Five checks for any candidate, for any office:

1. What have they actually done? Look at the record, not the promises.
2. A plan, or only a slogan? Ask how, with what money and by when.
3. Who is on the team? Running mates and appointees do much of the work.
4. Do they face hard questions? Watch debates and interviews.
5. Do the numbers add up? Big promises need real money.

Run all five on every candidate you're considering. Then choose for yourself.
